This is my Second try as a Voxel Engine. 
And before talking about anything concerning the project, I must send my highest
regards to [Lapinozz](https://github.com/lapinozz) who has helped me greatly with **motivation*, **using good practices** and even coding some more cumbersome parts of the engine.

## The (sad) story of the project

At first, I wanted this project to become a real game... yet, as the months went by, I quickly realised that it was not maintainable. As a student &mdash; and especially this year &mdash; I have got too tight of a schedule to work on this project on a daily basis. Which is highly frustrating since I must sometimes take week-long breaks. \
Furthermore, were I to take this project where I originally wanted to go, I would need another few years. Which sadly, I can't afford. 

Therefore, I have chosen to put a stop to it (quite regrettably). I shall perhaps come back to it later on. For the time being, I will put more focus into projects at a smaller scale &mdash; which I can actually finish &mdash; and that have me learn more over a shorter period of time. As was the original goal behind this project.

Feel free to follow my other projects however!
